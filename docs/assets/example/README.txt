Original Beheader Web examples, GPL-3.0-only.
The image is a generated 32 by 24 raster with transparency; the MP3 is a generated 440 Hz tone.
English and Italian filenames, HTML, PDF and archive contents are defined in scripts/example-content.json.
Regenerate both localized sets and their loading manifest with python scripts/create-example-documents.py.
The image and instrumental tone have no language-specific text or speech; their bytes are shared and their selected filenames are translated.
English: archive-a.zip and archive-b.zip share hello.txt with different greetings.
Italian: archivio-a.zip and archivio-b.zip share ciao.txt with different greetings.
Each archive also contains a.txt or b.txt. Reordering changes the greeting; both unique files remain.
No third-party recording, artwork, or user content is included.
